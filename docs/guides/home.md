# Amche Atlas

Amche Atlas is a free digital library of public maps for Goa, India — cadastral plots, land-use zones, coastal regulation limits, hazard layers and more, kept current by volunteers from the [OpenStreetMap India](https://openstreetmap.in/) community. *Amche* means "ours" in Konkani, the language of the Konkan coast, and the atlas is built the same way it's named: a shared record anyone can read, question or extend.
